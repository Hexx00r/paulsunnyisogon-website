# 60-second Loom script

Record with their demo site open in one tab and their current site in another. Keep it under a minute.

| Time | Say | Show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00-0:12 | "Hi {{first_name}}, Paul here. I had a look at {{business}}'s site and the main thing I noticed is {{specific_problem: e.g. there's no way to get a price without calling, so after-hours enquiries go to the competitor who answers first}}." | Their current site, scroll to the contact/quote area. |
| 0:12-0:30 | "So I rebuilt it as a free demo. Here it is. This calculator lets a customer tick the services, type the area, and see an estimate straight away." | Demo site. Tick two services, enter an area, show the estimate. |
| 0:30-0:45 | "When they submit, it comes straight to your phone as a lead, and a text goes back to them, so you're not chasing it from the ladder. If they don't book, a follow-up goes out automatically." | Lead notification on phone / pipeline view. |
| 0:45-0:55 | "Setup is from $697 one-time while I take my first five clients, then $147 a month if you want me to keep it running." | Pricing section of paulsunnydev.com. |
| 0:55-1:00 | "If it's useful, book 15 minutes with me using the link below and I'll walk you through it. Cheers." | Booking link / your face. |

Before you hit record: confirm the demo shows their real business name, suburb and phone, and the
prices in the calculator are sensible for their area. Don't claim numbers you can't back up.
