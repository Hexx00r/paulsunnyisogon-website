## Rules

- No claims about results unless they are real and approved (no invented stats or testimonials).
- Do not send to anyone who has said no. Include a plain "reply 'stop' and I won't email again" line if
  you are emailing cold at volume (Spam Act 2003: identify yourself, give a way to opt out, and only
  email business addresses relevant to their role).
