Most pressure cleaning websites are digital business cards. Yours should quote the job in about 30 seconds, follow up by itself, and book work while you're on the truck — that's what I build. And it's not theory: the first business I built it for was my family's pressure cleaning company in Albury–Wodonga.

What a pressure cleaning website actually has to do

The difference between a $500 site and a $3,000 system isn't prettier photos. It's these five things:

  • Instant ballpark pricing. "Call for a quote" loses to the competitor who gives a number in 30 seconds. A simple per-m² calculator filters tyre-kickers and captures contact details at peak intent.
  • Before/after gallery. Your best salesperson. Phone photos are fine — the transformation is what sells.
  • Suburb-level pages. "Pressure cleaning Albury" beats a generic homepage when someone searches locally — this is how you show up outside hipages.
  • Google reviews pulled in automatically. Social proof without copying and pasting.
  • Follow-up automation. The lead who enquires at 8pm gets an SMS in 60 seconds, not a call-back tomorrow when they've already booked someone else. This alone is the difference between a website and a funnel.
