The problem with most pressure cleaning websites

Most tradie websites — including most of the ones agencies charge $3,000+ for — are digital business cards. They look fine, they list your services, and then they sit there. Meanwhile the things that actually cost you jobs keep happening: the 8pm enquiry that doesn't get answered until tomorrow morning, by which point the homeowner has booked someone else. The quote request that goes into a notebook, then gets forgotten after a long day on the wand. The $400 a month going to hipages for shared leads that two other cleaners got at the same time.

The website isn't the problem — what it doesn't do is. This page is the fix, and everything described here is running live, calculator included.

Questions owners ask me

How long does it take?

Seven days from the kickoff call, fixed — the process above is the whole process. Delays almost always come from one thing: the copy approval sitting in your inbox. Turn that around same-day and day 7 is launch day.

Do I have to use GoHighLevel?

The automation — pipeline, follow-up SMS, review requests — runs on GoHighLevel, and the calculator is built to drop leads straight into it. If you'd rather keep a different CRM, the site itself is portable; the automation would need rewiring, which we can quote separately. Most owners just start on GHL since the platform cost is already factored in.

Will it actually work on phones?

Designed for a phone first — over half your visitors will be standing in a driveway holding one. Every build gets tested at small-screen sizes before launch, and you're holding the proof: this page, on your phone, right now.

What if I get no leads after launch?

We find out why instead of guessing — the analytics show where visitors come from and where they drop off, and the audit includes a 30-day check-in to tune it. If the maths doesn't work for your market, I'll tell you that on the call, before you pay anything.

Do you only build for pressure cleaning?

Pressure cleaning is my niche — I know the services, the seasonality, and the hipages problem from the inside, because it's the family trade. The same system ports to other one-person trade businesses (the Melbourne build is the same system in a different skin), but if your business is far outside that world I'll say so and point you to someone better suited.
