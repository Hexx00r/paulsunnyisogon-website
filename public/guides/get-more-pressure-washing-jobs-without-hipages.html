How to Get More Pressure Washing Jobs Without hipages

Short answer: hipages works as a starter motor, not an engine. The pressure cleaning businesses that grow past it do five things: own a fast website with an instant quote calculator, work their Google Business Profile weekly, collect reviews automatically after every job, build suburb-level pages hipages can't rank for, and follow up every lead in 60 seconds with automation. None of this needs a marketing degree, it needs a system. Here's the whole playbook, in the order to do it.

Step 1: Google Business Profile (free, highest ROI)

"Pressure cleaning near me" puts the map pack above every other result, including hipages. Getting into that 3-pack is free:

  • Claim and verify your profile (you'll need your ABN details and a service area).
  • Primary category: "Pressure washing service". Set your service area by suburb.
  • Upload before/after photos from every job, taken on your phone, on-site.
  • Post a short update weekly ("Driveway restore in Wodonga this week 👇"). Ten minutes, signals you're active.

Step 2: Reviews on autopilot

Reviews are the tiebreaker for the map pack and the trust signal that converts the click. Rough AU benchmarks: 50+ reviews to compete in metro areas, 15–20 in regional. The system is simple:

  1. Job done, customer's happy, driveway gleaming.
  2. Automated SMS within the hour: "Thanks [name], if you're happy with the result, a quick Google review helps our family business heaps: [link]"
  3. One reminder two days later. Done.

Ask manually if you like, but automation means it happens after every job, including the ones where you're too knackered to remember. Never pay or discount for reviews; it violates Google's policies and risks your whole profile.

Step 3: A website that gives instant answers

Here's the uncomfortable truth about your own lead flow: when someone finds you on Google, you have about 30 seconds before they hit the back button and request a hipages quote instead. What keeps them:

  • An instant ballpark price. A calculator (driveway m² × your rate) beats "call for a quote" every time: the person enquiring at 8pm gets a number now, and you get their details.
  • Before/after photos above the fold. Proof before persuasion.
  • Fast on a phone. They're standing in the driveway on 4G. Heavy page-builder sites lose them.

Step 4: Suburb pages hipages can't touch

Hipages ranks for "pressure cleaning Melbourne". It does not rank for "pressure cleaning San Remo", but your future customers search exactly that. One page per priority suburb, each with a real job story, local photos, and a review from that area. Three to five pages to start. This is how a one-truck operation outranks a national platform: they can't be local everywhere; you already are.

Step 5: Follow up in 60 seconds, not tomorrow

When a lead comes through your own site, speed decides the job. Research across home services keeps showing the same thing: respond within minutes and your chance of winning the job multiplies; call back tomorrow and they've booked someone else. You can't answer the phone on a roof, but an automated SMS can:

  • Quote request lands → instant SMS: "Got it, [name], I'll confirm your fixed quote within one business day. Any photos of the area help: just reply here."
  • No response in 24h → one polite nudge.
  • Quote sent → automated follow-up sequence until they book or say no.

The realistic exit plan

  1. Months 1–2: keep hipages. Set up GBP, start the review habit, get the website live.
  2. Months 3–4: suburb pages start ranking; reviews pass 15–20. Track where each job comes from.
  3. Months 5–6: when owned leads cover your baseline, drop hipages to the cheapest tier, or off entirely.

Every month you wait, the subscription keeps building someone else's platform. Every month you run the system, it builds yours.

Questions owners ask about leaving hipages

Is hipages worth it for a new pressure cleaning business?

As a starter motor, yes, it gets a brand-new business its first jobs while you build lead flow you own. Treat it as temporary: use every hipages job to collect a Google review and a before/after photo, because those build your profile, not theirs. Go in with an exit date, not an open-ended subscription.

How long until my own leads replace hipages?

Plan for about six months. Months 1–2: set up your Google Business Profile, get the website live, start the review habit. Months 3–4: suburb pages start ranking and reviews pass 15–20. Months 5–6: when your own leads cover the baseline, drop hipages to the cheapest tier or off entirely.
