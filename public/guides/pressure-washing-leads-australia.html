Questions owners ask about pressure washing leads

Are hipages leads worth it for pressure washing?

As a short-term top-up, sometimes; as your main channel, rarely. hipages plans run $139–$649 a month depending on trade and area, with typical spend around $400 a month, and most leads are shared with two or three competitors, so you are paying full price for a race to the phone. They make sense while an owned website and Google Business Profile are still ramping up, but the cost never drops and the leads stop the day you stop paying.

What's the cheapest way to get pressure washing leads in Australia?

Google Business Profile plus a review system: the listing itself is free and it captures people searching with local intent right now. Referrals and repeat customers are even cheaper per job, but they are passive, so you need a habit of asking for reviews and referrals after every job. Both feed directly into an owned website, which is where the compounding happens.

How many leads does a website with an instant quote calculator get?

It depends on your suburbs, your reviews, and your search presence, so any single number would be made up. How long until owned leads beat hipages?

Count it in months, not weeks, and honestly it depends on your area. A Google Business Profile with steady reviews starts producing calls within the first couple of months; suburb pages and organic search take longer to mature. The difference is that every month of work on owned channels raises next month's result, while every month of hipages spend resets to zero, and the owned system keeps answering enquiries after you stop paying for ads.
